# Tool ideas

Things that might get built next. Effort is a rough guess: **S** is an evening,
**M** is a weekend, and **L** needs a backend or an external login. Improvements to
existing apps are on the [to-do list](/docs/todos).

## Top picks

1. **Meeting Cost Ticker (S).** Enter headcount and average salary, press start,
   and watch the money burn in real time. Share the final bill.
2. **Walkies Forecast (S).** Scores the next 12 hours for walking a dog, using
   temperature, rain and pavement heat. Open-Meteo needs no API key.

## Out in the world

- **Sunset Odds (S).** Rates tonight's sunset from the cloud forecast, and tells you
  when to leave the house and which way to face.
- **Drift Walk (S).** Rolls dice for your next move on a walk: "Turn left. Stop at
  the next red door. Ask a dog's name."
- **Road Trip Bingo (S).** Makes a different bingo card for each person in the car,
  with squares like "cow standing alone" and "suspiciously large fruit statue".
- **Your Own Constellation (S).** Draws a constellation from your name and birthday,
  then invents the ancient myth behind it. Printable.

## Home and people

- **The Seating Chart Dial (M).** Enter your guests and who's feuding, then slide from
  "peaceful dinner" to "maximum drama" and watch the table rearrange.
- **Petty Court (M).** Two people give their sides of a dispute, like who left the pan
  in the sink. A judge issues a formal ruling and a sentence.
- **Houseplant Witness Statement (S).** Your neglected plant gives sworn testimony
  about you, with dates. Includes a real watering reminder.
- **Museum of Ordinary Things (S).** Upload a photo of your stapler or an old sock and
  get a pompous museum placard to go with it.
- **Postcard From Your Dog (S).** Writes the postcard your dog would send about their
  day while you were at work. Printable and frankly accusatory.
- **Made-Up Holiday of the Day (S).** Every date gets an invented holiday, with its
  traditions, foods and a small way to celebrate it.

## Inspired by the internet

These build on formats that keep turning up online. APIs still need checking before
anything gets built.

- **Your Next Milestones (S).** Finds your next odd anniversaries, like 10,000 days old
  or a billion seconds, and adds them to your calendar. Inspired by fun life
  calculators.
- **Measured in Shepherds (S).** Compares anything, from a bus to a skyscraper, in
  German Shepherds, on a long scrolling scale. Inspired by Size of Space.
- **Tiny Dilemmas (L).** A daily awkward choice, like whether to tell a friend they
  have spinach in their teeth, then shows how everyone else voted. Inspired by Absurd
  Trolley Problems.
- **Mix-a-Breed (M).** Combine any two dogs, then keep combining the results, while AI
  names and describes each one. Inspired by Infinite Craft.
- **Where the Paycheck Goes (S).** Enter your income and bills and get a flow diagram,
  with nothing leaving your browser. Inspired by BudgetFlow.
- **How Far in 30 Minutes (M).** Shows how far you can get from an address, for apartment
  hunting or picking a meeting spot. Inspired by transit time maps, and needs a routing
  API.
- **Label Decoder (M).** Look up a packaged food and read its ingredients in plain
  English. Open Food Facts is listed as needing no API key.

## Just for fun

- **Good Boy Certificate Generator (S).** A printable, official-looking diploma for
  your dog.
- **Excuse Generator (S).** Plausible reasons for missing standup, with a
  believability score.
- **Bad Startup Pitch Machine (S).** "It's X for Y, but on the blockchain." Slide
  deck optional.
- **Bark Soundboard (S).** Every breed from the game, plus a panic button.
- **Which Breed Are You? (S).** A short quiz. It's rigged so everyone gets close to
  German Shepherd.
- **Guess the Breed (M).** A daily puzzle with five escalating clues and a shareable
  result.
- **Sad Dog Pomodoro (S).** A focus timer with a dog that gets visibly sadder every
  time you switch tabs.
- **Commit Message Roaster (M).** Paste your git log and get judged. The funniest
  version uses an LLM.

## Actually useful

- **Fridge Rescue (M).** List what's about to go bad and get meals that use it up,
  starting with whatever expires first.
- **Packing List Builder (S).** Enter the destination, dates and plans, and get a
  checklist that fits the forecast. Open-Meteo needs no API key.
- **Cost Per Use (S).** Enter a price and how often you'd really use something, then
  compare buying it, renting it and skipping it.
- **Home Upkeep Calendar (S).** Pick what you own, like a furnace or smoke alarms, and
  get a maintenance schedule to add to your calendar.
- **Recipe Scaler (S).** Change the servings or pan size, convert the units, and get
  sensible amounts instead of "1.33 eggs".
- **Regex Explainer (S).** Test a pattern and read it back in plain English.
- **Cron Translator (S).** Plain English in both directions, with the next ten run
  times.
- **JSON to TypeScript (S).** Paste a response and get types. It would have caught
  the Dota Tracker's stale API fields.
- **Time Zone Overlap (S).** Find the best meeting window across cities.
- **Split the Bill (S).** Handles uneven shares and who had the expensive steak, and
  gives you a link to share.
- **Palette From a Photo (S).** Pull colors from an image and check contrast.

## Building on what's here

- **Dota Draft Helper (M).** Suggest counters and show win rates against the enemy
  draft.
- **Dota Year in Review (M).** Turn a player's match history into a shareable recap.
- **Spotify Receipt (L).** Your top tracks as a printed shop receipt. It's a
  good first version of the planned Spotify Tracker, and needs Spotify login.
