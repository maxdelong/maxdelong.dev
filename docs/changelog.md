# Changelog

## November 2026

### Agentic Dev

- New guide to what's changing in agentic development, with recommended tools, a loop
  safety checklist and numbered sources. It was last checked on November 6, 2026.

### Five new apps

- **Daily Fetch:** one throw a day, with the same dog and wind for everyone.
- **Lunch Roulette:** spin your own list of places, and pay for your vetoes.
- **True Cost of a Car:** compare two cars by what they really cost.
- **Work Backward:** enter when you need to be there and find out when to start.
- **Emoji Search:** find any emoji by name, keyword or category, even with a typo, and
  copy it with one click.
- Every app now shows its to-do list, all gathered on one [to-do page](/docs/todos).

### Hero Roulette

- New app: spin a random Dota 2 hero, a loose game plan and a house rule. The first
  reroll is free, and the rest come with punishments.

### Site redesign and docs

- New compact design with a proper sidebar, app cards, the Geist font and a dark
  theme that follows your system.
- Added this docs section, published from Markdown files in the repo.

### Dog Tower Defense 2.0

- Seven breeds, with the German Shepherd as the best of them.
- Two upgrade paths per breed, and the Alpha Command ability.
- Twenty rounds, ten intruder types, hidden and prickly traits, spraying skunks and
  splitting bosses.
- Place dogs anywhere on the grass, choose who they target, sell them, and run the
  game at up to 3× speed.

### Maintenance

- Reworked the first version's wave pacing, which ended in under 3 seconds.
- Upgraded Next.js and other dependencies to resolve security advisories.
- Added CI and Dependabot.

## August 2026

- First version: site shell, Dota Tracker and a small Dog Tower Defense.
