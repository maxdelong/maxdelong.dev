# Dog Tower Defense field guide

Keep the intruders off the porch for twenty rounds. You start with 400 bones and
40 lives. Defeats earn bones, and every cleared round pays a bonus.

## How to play

1. Pick a dog from the shop, or press keys 1 to 7.
2. Click the grass to place it. Dogs can't stand on paths, the pond or the porch.
3. Press **Start round**. You can keep buying and upgrading while a wave runs.
4. Click a placed dog to upgrade it, change who it targets, or sell it for 70% of
   what you spent on it.

The **speed** button runs the game at up to 3×, and **Auto** starts each round for
you. Press Esc to cancel a placement.

## Breeds

| Breed | Cost | Role | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chihuahua | 90 | Cheap and furious | Bites very fast, but has tiny range. |
| Beagle | 130 | The nose | Sees hidden intruders from the start. |
| Corgi | 150 | Herder | Slows everything in range. Doesn't bite until upgraded. |
| Labrador | 200 | Long range | Tennis balls hit 3 intruders. |
| Golden Retriever | 250 | Support | Doesn't fight. Earns bones each round and can speed up nearby dogs. |
| Husky | 300 | Area damage | Howls hit everything in range, including prickly intruders. |
| German Shepherd | 500 | The best dog | Hardest hitter, bites 2 at once, sees hidden, ignores spines. |

The German Shepherd is the best breed, and the tests enforce that. It has the
highest damage per second at every upgrade level, and it's the only breed that can
win the game alone. You can't afford one on round 1, though.

## Upgrades

Every breed has two upgrade paths with three tiers each. **Only one path can go
past tier 1**, so a dog ends up at 3 and 1 at most, and you have to choose.

The Shepherd's top K-9 Unit upgrade unlocks **Alpha Command**, the only ability in
the game: every dog in the yard attacks three times as fast for 8 seconds, then it
recharges for 40 seconds.

## Tips

- The round preview under the yard warns you when hidden or prickly intruders are
  coming. Cats arrive on round 5 and hedgehogs on round 7.
- The two paths meet near the porch, so dogs placed there cover both.
- Keep dogs back from the path where skunks walk, or take them out from long range.
- A Golden Retriever bought early pays for itself quickly.
- Set a long-range dog to **Strong** when a boss is coming.
